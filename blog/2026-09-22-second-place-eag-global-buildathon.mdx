on september 20, 2026, at the auditorio sidoc of universidad icesi in cali,
they announced the winners of the **EAG Global Buildathon** — the ethcali
builders tour stop — and i got **2nd place** with **Áureo**.

{/* truncate */}

25+ hackers, 13 projects, less than 48 hours. the track was *application
middleware & open-source tooling* and the prize was 200 usdt paid on ethereum
mainnet.

## the project: aureo

áureo is open-source ethereum middleware for risk and compliance. it watches
corporate transfers registered on chain and evaluates them in real time with
deterministic speed and volume rules. then it returns a risk verdict —
**high / medium / low** — through cli, api and websocket, with the evidence
auditable behind it.

short version: it is a camera in front of *your* building, not in front of the
whole city. it only watches the contract you configure, and it tells you when
something looks off.

## how the weekend actually went

- **friday night**: repo, `AureoCore.sol`, hardhat + ignition, one contract
  with roles and a circuit breaker.
- **saturday**: the backend ingesting logs over rpc, a 60s analysis window and
  the two deterministic rules (too many ops in a few blocks, absurd volume).
- **saturday night**: websocket `/stream`, cli and the audit report. this is
  where sleep stopped being a priority.
- **sunday**: docs, `npm run docker:up` so a judge could run the whole thing
  with one command, and a demo dapp to show the sdk live.

## what i take from it

honestly, the win was not the money. it was that the thing they clicked
**worked on their machine** — no "clone it and pray" moment. everything was
scripted: `setup:env`, `docker:up`, `cli stream`.

also: i'm 18 and i've been writing code since i was 7. seeing my name on that
list next to people i follow was a strange, very good feeling.
